# Evidence gaps and interpretation

Snapshot date: 2026-09-28. This is a small public-source demonstration, not a complete inventory of education or cultural-program sites in Ghana.

- **American Spaces status:** an official historical newsletter mentions the American Center and an American Corner in Accra. The sources collected here do not establish whether either currently operates. Both site statuses are `unknown`; that value does not mean closed.
- **EducationUSA status:** the three centers are listed in current official EducationUSA pages/directories as viewed for this snapshot. The pages do not provide source-backed opening or closure dates, so lifecycle dates are blank.
- **Map precision:** official pages supply two Accra street addresses, but the point lookup did not resolve those buildings. Accra and Kumasi locations therefore use city reference points; University of Ghana and CIUG use a campus reference point. These points are not exact service venues.
- **External institution:** Zhejiang University of Technology is included because the University of Ghana source names it as a CIUG partner. This sample does not verify the partner's address, coordinates, operating status, or the full scope and dates of the relationship.
- **Coverage:** this sample contains selected Accra and Kumasi locations plus one university partnership. It does not establish national coverage, comparative reach, program outcomes, influence, or absence of other sites.
- **Review state:** imported source claims, relationships, and map-location claims remain unreviewed. The bundle preserves source links so a reviewer can inspect each claim.
